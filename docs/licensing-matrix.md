# Data-source licensing matrix

Verified 25 November 2026. A blank cell means "not established", which is treated as NO.
Nothing in this repository publishes third-party raw data or derived artifacts.

| Source | Research / offline experimentation | Production model training | Redistribution of raw or derived data | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Synthetic corpus (this repo)** | YES | YES | YES | Generated by `rsil.providers.synthetic`, CC0-1.0, no real clubs or prices |
| **StatsBomb / Hudl Open Data** | YES with attribution | NO | NO | Free for research and public analysis; must not imply endorsement; commercial use needs a licence |
| **Football-Data.co.uk CSV** | BLOCKED | NO | NO | Its published policy excludes commercial or AI data-training projects involving automated bots/scrapers. Written permission required before any automated collection |
| **football-data.org** | YES within the free-tier terms | NOT ESTABLISHED | NO | Free tier is rate limited; deeper history is paid. Ask before production training |
| **API-Football (api-sports)** | YES on a paid plan | PLAN DEPENDENT | NO | Free tier is for evaluation; production training needs the paid terms in writing |
| **TheOddsAPI** | Ron's existing quota only | NO | NO | Ron holds the subscription; the lab must not open a second one |
| **Opta / Stats Perform, Sportmonks** | Commercial licence only | Commercial licence only | NO | Out of scope until revenue justifies it |

## Standing blockers

1. **Football-Data.co.uk is not a permitted corpus source.** No adapter for it exists, no
   automated collection is performed, and none may be added without written permission
   recorded in the manifest metadata.
2. **StatsBomb open data may be used for research only.** Any artifact derived from it
   stays non-commercial and is never published until redistribution rights are established.
3. **No raw third-party data and no derived artifacts are published** from this repository.
   Only the synthetic corpus and code are published.

## How permission is recorded

Every dataset manifest carries `source.licence`, `source.licenceUrl`,
`source.attribution` and `source.permitted.{research,production_training,redistribution}`.
`rsil.corpus.build` refuses any source whose `research` permission is not true, and every
manifest states `suitableForProductionTraining: false` until a source is explicitly cleared.
